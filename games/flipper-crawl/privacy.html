{{title}} is a mobile game made by {{studio}} ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game collects (in short: nothing), what stays on your device, and the choices you have. Questions: {{email}}.

In short

  • There's no account. Your progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The game has no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it makes no network connections of its own.
  • We don't collect, receive, sell or share any personal information.
  • If the game offers in-app purchases, Google Play handles them. We never see your payment details.

What stays on your device

Your progress (realms cleared, stars, Commander level, Embers and gems, research, ball cores, spells, Keep upgrades, quests, achievements and the monster Codex), your settings and any record of purchases are saved in the game's own storage on your device, with a backup copy so a crash can't corrupt them. They aren't sent to us, so we can't see, back up or restore them. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes them. Android's own device backup, if you have it turned on, may include the game's storage under Google's terms.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

If the game offers in-app purchases, they're made through Google Play. The game receives only the item bought, an order ID and a purchase token, checks the purchase and delivers it, and keeps a record of it in your local save. We never see your name, card or payment details. Google handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. If a future version adds anything else that sends data off your device, we'll update this policy before that version ships.

Permissions

The game asks Android only for vibration (for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings). It doesn't use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location, and it doesn't send notifications.

Who we share it with

No one: the game doesn't send us anything to share. Google Play processes purchases, as described above.

How long it's kept

Your save stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its storage. If you email us, we keep the conversation only as long as we need to help you.
