In short

  • There's no account. Your progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads you choose to watch, and occasional full-screen ad breaks when you leave a run's results screen (from your 4th run, at most every 2nd run and 3 minutes apart). Never during a run, and no banner ads.
  • AdMob uses data such as your device's advertising ID. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before ads load, and you can change your choice any time.
  • Google Play handles purchases. We never see your payment details.
  • The game has no analytics of its own and sends no usage statistics to us.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

What stays on your device

Your progress (best distance, checkpoints reached, coins, gems and hero cards, upgrades, heroes, weapons, daily missions and achievements), your settings and the record of purchases you've made are saved in the game's own storage on your device, with a backup copy so a crash can't corrupt them. They aren't sent to us, so we can't see, back up or restore them. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes them. Android's own device backup, if you have it turned on, may include the game's storage under Google's terms.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows two kinds of ads through Google AdMob:

  • Rewarded ads: optional ads you choose to watch for a reward: reviving your hero once a run, doubling the coins from a run, a boosted start, opening a supply chest early, re-rolling a daily mission, or a few free gems in the store (three times a day).
  • Interstitial ads: occasional full-screen ad breaks when you leave a run's results screen, starting from your 4th run and not in your first 10 minutes, at most every 2nd run and at least 3 minutes apart. Never during a run. Players who have bought anything in the game, or who have an active Commander Pass, never get them.

The game doesn't show banner ads, and ads are limited to content suitable for general and parental-guidance audiences.

To show, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, device and app information (such as the model, operating system, language and app version), your IP address and the approximate location it implies, crash and performance information about the ads SDK, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this under its own privacy policy; see how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Remove Ads: buying Remove Ads turns ad breaks off completely. Rewarded ads stay available as your own choice. The Commander Pass also turns ad breaks off while it is active, and gives some rewards without an ad.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): where the law requires it, Google's consent tool asks whether ads may use your data before any ad loads. You can say no and still play; you'll see non-personalized or limited ads.
  • US state privacy rights: if you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (such as California, Virginia, Colorado or Connecticut), you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising, using the ad privacy choices in the game where they're shown, or by turning off ad personalization on your device.
  • Change your mind any time: where your choices apply, Settings ▸ Privacy options in the game lets you review or change them.
  • Your device settings: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization (Settings ▸ Google ▸ Ads, or Settings ▸ Security & privacy ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads, depending on your device).

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases are made through Google Play: one-time items (premium heroes, the Starter Pack and Remove Ads), gem packs, and the optional Commander Pass subscription. Gems can also be earned by playing, and all non-premium content can be earned without paying. Nothing you buy is random: there are no paid loot boxes. The game receives the item bought, an order ID and a purchase token, checks the purchase and delivers it, and keeps a record of it in your local save; items and subscriptions you own are restored from Google Play when the game starts. We never see your name, card or payment details. Google handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy.

Commander Pass (subscription)

The Commander Pass is a weekly or monthly auto-renewing subscription sold through Google Play. The price and billing period are shown before you subscribe. It renews automatically until you cancel; you can cancel any time in the Google Play app (Profile ▸ Payments & subscriptions ▸ Subscriptions), and it stays active until the end of the period you paid for. The game checks the subscription with Google Play to know whether its perks are active; it doesn't send anything about it to us.

Usage statistics

{{name}} doesn't include an analytics SDK of our own and doesn't send usage statistics or crash reports to us. If a future version adds any, we'll update this policy before that version ships.

Permissions

The game asks Android for internet and network-state access (for ads and purchases), the advertising ID (for ads, added by Google's ads SDK), Google Play billing, and vibration (for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings). Google's SDKs also add a few routine permissions they need to work (such as keeping the device awake during a purchase). The game doesn't use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location, and it doesn't send notifications.

Who we share it with

  • Google serves ads (AdMob) and processes purchases (Google Play), as described above.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

We don't sell your personal information for money. In some places the law counts advertising that uses data like your advertising ID as "selling" or "sharing"; the ad choices above let you opt out of it.

How long it's kept

Your save stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its storage. Google keeps ads and purchase data according to its own policies. If you email us, we keep the conversation only as long as we need to help you.
