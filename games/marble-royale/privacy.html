{{name}} is a mobile game made by {{studio}} ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game collects, why, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. Questions: {{email}}.

In short

  • Your progress and settings are saved on your device.
  • Online play (races against other players' recorded runs, friends, challenges, ranked leagues) uses an anonymous account on our game server, tied to a random ID for your device. There's no e-mail, password or sign-in.
  • The game shows no ads and contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. It doesn't use your advertising ID.
  • Purchases, when the store offers them, go through Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • You can delete your online account and everything linked to it from the game's Settings at any time.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

What stays on your device

Your progress (coins, gems, XP, unlocked marbles, skins, trails, shatter effects, gadgets, quests, trophies and settings) is saved in the game's own storage on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it. If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android may back up the game's storage to your Google account under Google's terms; we can't see that backup.

Your online account

The first time the game goes online, it creates an account on our game server using a random identifier generated for your device installation (a device ID). We use it only to recognise your account; it isn't your advertising ID. To run online play, the server stores:

  • Your racer name, chosen by you (and changeable with Rename in Settings), and a friend code the game gives you. Other players can see your racer name.
  • Friends and challenges: the players you add by friend code or name, challenges you send and receive, and invite codes you redeem.
  • Match records: your places, scores, knockouts, trophies, league, season progress and rewards, so the server can rank matches and prevent cheating.
  • Recorded runs ("ghosts"): a compact recording of how your marble moved in a race (up to 2 KB each). Your recorded runs race as rivals in other players' matches, shown with your racer name. The server keeps only your 5 most recent, none older than 14 days.
  • Ghost reports: what your recorded runs did in other players' matches, and the coins they earned you.
  • Technical data our server receives with every connection: your IP address, the time, and the game version. Server logs are used to keep the service running and secure.

The game doesn't ask for your real name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts or location.

Deleting your account and data

Open Settings ▸ Delete online account in the game and confirm. This deletes your online account and everything linked to it on our server straight away: racer name, friend code, friends, challenges, recorded runs, match records, ranked history, ghost reports and trophies stored online. It can't be undone. Progress saved on your phone stays there until you uninstall the game or clear its storage.

If you can't open the game, email {{email}} with your racer name and friend code (shown in the game's Friends screen) and we'll delete the account for you within 30 days. Server backups that still contain deleted data are overwritten within 30 days.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

If the game's store offers items for real money, they're bought through Google Play. The game receives the item bought, an order ID and a purchase token, verifies the purchase and delivers it, and keeps a record of it so it isn't delivered twice. We never see your name, card or payment details; Google handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. Purchases are optional: everything that affects how a race plays can be earned by playing.

Permissions

The game asks Android for internet and network-state access (for online play) and vibration (for haptic feedback). It reads your phone's motion sensors on the device to steer the marble when you tilt the phone; that sensor data isn't stored or sent anywhere. The game doesn't use your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Who we share it with

  • Other players see your racer name, your recorded runs, your league and trophies, and what you share with friends.
  • Our hosting provider runs the server that stores the online account data above, on our behalf.
  • Google processes purchases (Google Play) and, if Android backup is on, stores your backup.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

How long it's kept

Your online account data is kept while your account exists, except recorded runs (your latest 5, at most 14 days). If an account isn't used for 24 months we may delete it. Deleting your account removes the data straight away (backups within 30 days). Your on-device save stays until you uninstall the game or clear its storage. If you email us, we keep the conversation only as long as we need to help you.

Children

{{name}} is meant for players aged 13 and over and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an online account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, or to object to or restrict how it's used. You can rename or delete your online account yourself in Settings. For anything else, email {{email}} with your racer name and friend code; we'll answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Keeping it safe

The game talks to our server over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Only we can access the server. Match results and purchases are checked on the server before they count.

Changes
