{{name}} is a mobile game made by {{studio}} ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game collects, why, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. Questions: {{email}}.

In short

  • Your progress and settings are saved on your device.
  • Online play (races against other players' recorded runs, friends, challenges, ranked leagues) uses an anonymous account on our game server, tied to a random ID for your device. There's no e-mail, password or sign-in.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads you choose to watch, and occasional full-screen ad breaks between finished runs. Never during a race, and no banner ads. Remove Ads turns them off.
  • AdMob uses data such as your device's advertising ID. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before ads load, and you can change your choice any time.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game has no analytics SDK.
  • You can delete your online account and everything linked to it from the game's Settings at any time.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

What stays on your device

Your progress (coins, gems, XP, unlocked marbles, skins, trails, shatter effects, gadgets, quests, trophies and settings) is saved in the game's own storage on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it. If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android may back up the game's storage to your Google account under Google's terms; we can't see that backup.

Your online account

The first time the game goes online, it creates an account on our game server using a random identifier generated for your device installation (a device ID). We use it only to recognise your account; it isn't your advertising ID. To run online play, the server stores:

  • Your racer name, chosen by you (and changeable with Rename in Settings), and a friend code the game gives you. Other players can see your racer name.
  • Friends and challenges: the players you add by friend code or name, challenges you send and receive, and invite codes you redeem.
  • Match records: your places, scores, knockouts, trophies, league, season progress and rewards, so the server can rank matches and prevent cheating.
  • Recorded runs ("ghosts"): a compact recording of how your marble moved in a race (up to 2 KB each). Your recorded runs race as rivals in other players' matches, shown with your racer name. The server keeps only your 5 most recent, none older than 14 days.
  • Ghost reports: what your recorded runs did in other players' matches, and the coins they earned you.
  • Technical data our server receives with every connection: your IP address, the time, and the game version. Server logs are used to keep the service running and secure.

The game doesn't ask for your real name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts or location.

Deleting your account and data

Open Settings ▸ Delete online account in the game and confirm. This deletes your online account and everything linked to it on our server straight away: racer name, friend code, friends, challenges, recorded runs, match records, ranked history, ghost reports and trophies stored online. It can't be undone. Progress saved on your phone stays there until you uninstall the game or clear its storage.

If you can't open the game, email {{email}} with your racer name and friend code (shown in the game's Friends screen) and we'll delete the account for you within 30 days. Server backups that still contain deleted data are overwritten within 30 days.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version of the game shows two kinds of ads through Google AdMob:

  • Rewarded ads: optional ads you choose to watch for a reward: double coins after a run, a free coin gift, a doubled daily gift or an extra quest swap. Each has a daily limit.
  • Ad breaks (interstitial ads): an occasional full-screen ad as you leave a finished run's results, at most one every few minutes of play. Never during a race, never when you leave a race early, and none in your first few runs.

The game doesn't show banner ads. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

To show, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, device and app information (such as the model, operating system, language and app version), your IP address and the approximate location it implies, diagnostics, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this under its own privacy policy; see how Google uses information from apps that use its services. We don't receive this data from Google, and we don't link it to your online account.

Remove Ads: buying Remove Ads turns ad breaks off for good, and rewards that normally come with a rewarded ad are given to you straight away without one. With Remove Ads, the game stops requesting ads from AdMob.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): where the law requires it, Google's consent tool asks whether ads may use your data before any ad loads. You can say no and still play; you'll see non-personalized or limited ads.
  • US state privacy rights: if you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (such as California, Virginia, Colorado or Connecticut), you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising. Use Ad privacy choices in the game's Settings where it's shown, or turn off ad personalization on your device.
  • Change your mind any time: open Settings ▸ Ad privacy choices in the game to review or change your choices wherever they apply.
  • Your device settings: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization (Settings ▸ Google ▸ Ads, or Settings ▸ Security & privacy ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads, depending on your device).

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases are one-time purchases made through Google Play: premium marble skins, trails and shatter effects, themed sets, and Remove Ads. They're cosmetic or remove ads; nothing you buy changes how a race plays. The game receives the item bought, an order ID and a purchase token, checks the purchase with Google Play, delivers it, and keeps a record of it on your device so it isn't delivered twice. Every time the game starts (and from Settings ▸ Restore purchases) it asks Google Play what your account owns, so a reinstall or a new phone gets your items back. We never see your name, card or payment details; Google handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy.

Permissions

The game asks Android for internet and network-state access (for online play, ads and purchases), the advertising ID permission (for AdMob, as above), Google Play's billing permission, and vibration (for haptic feedback). It reads your phone's motion sensors on the device to steer the marble when you tilt the phone; that sensor data isn't stored or sent anywhere. The game doesn't use your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Who we share it with

  • Other players see your racer name, your recorded runs, your league and trophies, and what you share with friends.
  • Our hosting provider runs the server that stores the online account data above, on our behalf.
  • Google serves ads (AdMob) and processes purchases (Google Play), as described above, and, if Android backup is on, stores your backup.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

We don't sell your personal information for money, and your online account data isn't used for advertising. In some places the law counts advertising that uses data like your advertising ID as "selling" or "sharing"; the ad choices above let you opt out of it.

How long it's kept

Your online account data is kept while your account exists, except recorded runs (your latest 5, at most 14 days). If an account isn't used for 24 months we may delete it. Deleting your account removes the data straight away (backups within 30 days). Your on-device save stays until you uninstall the game or clear its storage. If you email us, we keep the conversation only as long as we need to help you.
