What stays on your device

Your progress (cash, Storm Cells, truck upgrades, crews, research, unlocks, paints, records and achievements), your settings and the record of purchases you've made are saved in the game's own storage on your device. They aren't sent to us: we don't run servers for {{name}}, so we can't see them. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes them. If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android backs up the game's save file (only that file) to your Google account and can restore it on a new phone or after a reinstall, under Google's terms; we can't see that backup. If the save file on your phone is ever damaged, the game keeps a copy of it on your phone so support can try to recover it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version of the game shows two kinds of ads through Google AdMob:

  • Interstitial ads: full-screen ads shown between chases now and then (never during a chase, and not until you've played a few chases).
  • Rewarded ads: optional ads you choose to watch for a reward, for example doubling a haul or repairing the truck.

So that an ad is ready when it's needed, the Google Mobile Ads SDK requests ads in the background.

To show, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, device and app information (such as the model, operating system, language and app version), your IP address and the approximate location it implies, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this under its own privacy policy; see how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Remove Ads: buying the Remove Ads purchase (or an active Chaser's License) stops the interstitial ads between chases. Rewarded ads stay available because they're always your choice; if you don't watch one, no ad is shown.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): after your first chase (the tutorial), before any ad is requested, Google's consent tool asks whether ads may use your data. You can say no and still play; you'll see non-personalized or limited ads.
  • US state privacy rights: if you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (such as California, Virginia, Colorado or Connecticut), you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising through the same consent tool.
  • Change your mind any time: open Setup ▸ Privacy options in the game to review or change your ad privacy choices (the button shows wherever the law requires it).
  • Your device settings: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization (Settings ▸ Google ▸ Ads, or Settings ▸ Security & privacy ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads, depending on your device).

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases (for example Remove Ads, Storm Cell packs, the Starter Pack and other packs, the Tempest vehicle, the Storm Pass and the Chaser's License subscription) are made through Google Play. The game receives the item bought, an order ID and a purchase token, checks the purchase on your device and delivers it; it keeps a record of the purchase in your local save so the item isn't delivered twice and can be restored. We never see your name, card or payment details. Google handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. Purchases are optional: nothing in the game requires one to progress. If Google Play refunds a purchase or it is charged back, the game removes the permanent item it gave (for example Remove Ads); items you already used stay used.

Subscription (Chaser's License)

The Chaser's License is a monthly subscription sold through Google Play. It renews automatically each month at the price shown in Google Play until you cancel it in Google Play (Play Store ▸ Payments & subscriptions ▸ Subscriptions, or MANAGE in the game's store). Cancelling stops the next renewal; the License stays on until the end of the period you paid for. The game learns from Google Play only whether it is active.

Storm Pass

Each Storm Pass season lasts 28 days. Buying a season's pass opens that season's premium reward track; rewards you earned but didn't claim are given to you automatically when the season ends. A pass applies to its own season only.

Reminders

If you turn them on (they're off by default), the game can remind you when your crews' haul is ready or a daily reward is waiting. These notifications are scheduled on your device and send nothing to us. Turn them off in the game's Setup or in your phone's settings.

Sharing a shot

The game can save a picture of your best moment on your device. If a future version lets you share it, your phone's share sheet will open and the picture will go only to the app and people you choose. Nothing is uploaded to us.

Usage statistics, crash and performance reports (Firebase)

Crash and performance reports. To find and fix crashes and slow loading, the Android game sends crash and performance reports to Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring, services of Google LLC. A report contains:

  • what went wrong: the crash or error, the program code that was running, and a short log of what the game was doing (for example which screen was open, or which storm and region a chase was in);
  • the device model, Android version, available memory, the graphics chip and the game's graphics settings, the game version and language;
  • load times: app start, reaching the home screen, loading a chase;
  • a random installation ID created by Firebase, and your IP address, which Firebase uses to estimate your country.

This is on by default. You can turn it off at any time in Setup ▸ Privacy & purchases ▸ Send crash reports; reports that haven't been sent yet are then deleted.

Usage statistics (you can turn them off). Unless you turn off Setup ▸ Privacy & purchases ▸ Share usage data (it's on until you do), and, where the law asks for it, only after you agree in Google's consent form, the game sends anonymous gameplay events to Google Analytics for Firebase: for example that a chase started or ended and how it went, upgrades and items bought with in-game money, purchases made, ads offered and watched, missions and Storm Pass progress. Firebase adds its own automatic events (first open, sessions, time in the app, app and Android updates) with the device model, Android version, game version, language, and your country (estimated from your IP address). Each install gets a random app-instance ID. We don't send or collect your advertising ID through Analytics, and nothing is used for ad personalization. Turning the switch off stops the statistics and deletes this install's Analytics ID.

Reports and statistics never contain your name, e-mail address, contacts, precise location or payment details. Firebase keeps crash reports for 90 days and performance data for up to 60 days; usage statistics are kept for 2 months (our Google Analytics retention setting) and then deleted. Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase data processing terms and its privacy policy. We use it only to fix and balance the game and never sell it.

Permissions

The game asks Android for internet and network-state access (for ads, purchases and the reports above), vibration (for haptic feedback) and, only if you turn reminders on, permission to post notifications. Google's Firebase SDK also reads which Play Store listing or campaign led to the install (the Play Install Referrer). The game doesn't use your camera, microphone, contacts or precise location.

Who we share it with

  • Google serves ads (AdMob), processes purchases (Google Play), receives the crash and performance reports and, if you allow it, the usage statistics and processes them for us (Firebase), as described above, and stores the backup of your save if Android backup is on.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

We don't sell your personal information for money. In some places the law counts advertising that uses data like your advertising ID as "selling" or "sharing"; the ad choices above let you opt out of it.

Your choices about statistics and reports

  • Setup ▸ Share usage data: on by default; turning it off stops usage statistics and deletes this phone's Analytics ID.
  • Setup ▸ Send crash reports: on by default; turning it off stops crash and performance reports and deletes the ones not yet sent.
