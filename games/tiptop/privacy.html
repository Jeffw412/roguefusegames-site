{{title}} is a mobile game made by {{studio}} ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game collects, why, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. Questions: {{email}}.

In short

  • There's no account. Your progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • Leaderboards and achievements use Google Play Games. If you're signed in to Play Games, Google gives the game your Play Games player ID and display name, and your scores and achievements go to Google.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads you choose to watch, and occasional full-screen ad breaks between plays from level 6 (at most one every 5 plays or 5 minutes of play). Never during a level or a Daily Puzzle, Blitz or Endless run, and no banner ads.
  • AdMob uses data such as your device's advertising ID. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before ads load, and you can change your choice any time.
  • Google Play handles purchases. We never see your payment details.
  • The game has no analytics of its own and sends no usage statistics to us.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels, coins, boosters, streaks, quests, trophies and unlocked cosmetics), your settings and the record of purchases you've made are saved in the game's own storage on your device. They aren't sent to us, so we can't see, back up or restore them. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes them. Android's own device backup, if you have it turned on, may include the game's storage under Google's terms.

Leaderboards and achievements (Google Play Games)

{{name}} uses Google Play Games Services for leaderboards and achievements. If you're signed in to Google Play Games on your device:

  • Google provides the game with your Play Games player ID and display name.
  • Your scores and achievements are sent to Google so they can be shown on leaderboards and in your Play Games profile.
  • Leaderboard entries (your display name and score) can be seen by other players, according to your Play Games visibility settings.

Google handles this data under its own privacy policy. You can manage your Play Games profile and who can see it in your Google Play Games settings. If you aren't signed in to Play Games, nothing is sent for leaderboards or achievements.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version of the game shows two kinds of ads through Google AdMob:

  • Rewarded ads: optional ads you choose to watch for a reward, for example extra tips, a free booster, double coins or saving a streak.
  • Interstitial ads: occasional full-screen ad breaks between plays, starting at level 6, at most one every 5 plays or 5 minutes of play. Never during a level or a Daily Puzzle, Blitz or Endless run.

The game doesn't show banner ads.

To show, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, device and app information (such as the model, operating system, language and app version), your IP address and the approximate location it implies, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this under its own privacy policy; see how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Remove Ads: buying Remove Ads (or "Everything", which includes it) turns ads off completely: no more ad breaks, and rewards that normally come with a rewarded ad are given to you straight away without one. With ads off, the game stops requesting ads from AdMob.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): where the law requires it, Google's consent tool asks whether ads may use your data before any ad loads. You can say no and still play; you'll see non-personalized or limited ads.
  • US state privacy rights: if you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (such as California, Virginia, Colorado or Connecticut), you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising. Use the ad privacy choices in the game's Settings ▸ Privacy where they're shown, or turn off ad personalization on your device (Android Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads, or delete your advertising ID).
  • Change your mind any time: open Settings ▸ Privacy in the game to review or change your ad privacy choices wherever they apply.
  • Your device settings: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization (Settings ▸ Google ▸ Ads, or Settings ▸ Security & privacy ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads, depending on your device).

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases are one-time purchases made through Google Play: premium tile sets, boards and backgrounds, themed bundles, Remove Ads and "Everything". They're cosmetic or remove ads; nothing you buy is needed to progress. There are no loot boxes and no subscriptions, and coins can only be earned by playing, never bought. The game receives the item bought, an order ID and a purchase token, checks the purchase and delivers it, and keeps a record of it in your local save so it can be restored with Restore purchases. We never see your name, card or payment details. Google handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy.

Sharing your Daily Puzzle result

If you tap Share on your Daily Puzzle result, your phone's share sheet opens and the result goes only to the app and people you choose. Nothing is uploaded to us.

Usage statistics

{{name}} doesn't include an analytics SDK of our own and doesn't send usage statistics or crash reports to us. If a future version adds any, we'll update this policy before that version ships.

Permissions

The game asks Android for internet and network-state access (for ads, purchases and Google Play Games), the advertising ID (for ads, added by Google's ads SDK), Google Play billing, and vibration (for haptic feedback). It doesn't use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location, and it doesn't send notifications.

Who we share it with

  • Google runs leaderboards and achievements (Google Play Games), serves ads (AdMob) and processes purchases (Google Play), as described above.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

We don't sell your personal information for money. In some places the law counts advertising that uses data like your advertising ID as "selling" or "sharing"; the ad choices above let you opt out of it.

How long it's kept

Your save stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its storage. Google keeps Play Games, ads and purchase data according to its own policies. If you email us, we keep the conversation only as long as we need to help you.
