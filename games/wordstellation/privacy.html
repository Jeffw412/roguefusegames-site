{{title}} is a mobile game made by {{studio}} ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game collects, why, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. Questions: {{email}}.

In short

  • There's no account. Your progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • Leaderboards and achievements use Google Play Games, and signing in is optional. If you're signed in, Google gives the game your Play Games player ID and display name, and your scores and achievements go to Google.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads you choose to watch, and occasional full-screen ad breaks between boards after your first 20 levels. Never during a puzzle, and no banner ads.
  • AdMob uses data such as your device's advertising ID. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before ads load, and you can change your choice any time.
  • Google Play handles purchases. We never see your payment details.
  • The game has no analytics of its own and sends no usage statistics to us.
  • Friend challenges you share contain only the board, your time, an optional nickname and a random install number, and they go only where you send them.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

What stays on your device

Your progress (Journey level, Stardust, Telescopes, the themes you've played, your Star Atlas, Daily Sky results and streak, solve times, and challenges you've sent or received), your settings and the record of purchases you've made are saved in the game's own storage on your device. They aren't sent to us, so we can't see, back up or restore them. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes them. Android's own device backup, if you have it turned on, may include the game's storage under Google's terms.

Puzzles are made on your device. The game doesn't download boards or send the words you trace anywhere.

Leaderboards and achievements (Google Play Games)

{{name}} can use Google Play Games Services for leaderboards and achievements. Signing in is optional, and you can play everything without it. If you're signed in to Google Play Games on your device:

  • Google provides the game with your Play Games player ID and display name.
  • Your scores and achievements are sent to Google so they can be shown on leaderboards and in your Play Games profile.
  • Leaderboard entries (your display name and score) can be seen by other players, according to your Play Games visibility settings.

Google handles this data under its own privacy policy. You can manage or delete your Play Games profile and its data, and choose who can see it, in your Google Play Games settings. If you aren't signed in to Play Games, nothing is sent for leaderboards or achievements.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version of the game shows two kinds of ads through Google AdMob:

  • Rewarded ads: optional ads you choose to watch by pressing a button, for a reward such as a free Telescope hint, double Stardust on a win, or keeping a Daily Sky streak after a missed day.
  • Interstitial ads: occasional full-screen ad breaks between boards. None in your first 20 levels; after that, at most one break per three finished boards or per four minutes, and only after a puzzle is solved. Never during a puzzle.

The game doesn't show banner ads.

To show, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, device and app information (such as the model, operating system, language and app version), your IP address and the approximate location it implies, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this under its own privacy policy; see how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Remove Ads: buying Remove Ads (or the Stargazer bundle, which includes it) turns ads off completely: no more ad breaks and no upsell pop-ups, and rewards that normally come with a rewarded ad are given to you straight away without one. With ads off, the game stops requesting ads from AdMob.

If you're offline, no ads are requested or shown.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): where the law requires it, Google's consent tool (the User Messaging Platform) asks whether ads may use your data before any ad loads. You can say no and still play; you'll see non-personalized or limited ads.
  • US state privacy rights: if you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (such as California, Virginia, Colorado or Connecticut), you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising. Use the ad privacy choices in the game's Settings ▸ Privacy options where they're shown, or turn off ad personalization on your device.
  • Change your mind any time: open Settings ▸ Privacy options in the game to review or change your ad privacy choices wherever they apply.
  • Your device settings: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization (Settings ▸ Google ▸ Ads, or Settings ▸ Security & privacy ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads, depending on your device).

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases are made through Google Play: Remove Ads, packs of Telescope hints, cosmetic sky themes and bundles. Nothing you buy is needed to finish a puzzle or to progress: Telescopes can also be earned by playing. There are no loot boxes and no subscriptions. The game receives the item bought, an order ID and a purchase token, checks the purchase and delivers it, and keeps a record of it in your local save so it can be restored with Restore purchases. We never see your name, card or payment details. Google handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy.

Sharing your Daily Sky result

If you tap Share on your Daily Sky result, your phone's share sheet opens and the result (an emoji grid that doesn't spoil the answers) goes only to the app and people you choose. Nothing is uploaded to us.

Solve times and friend challenges

The game records how long you take to solve each board and keeps those times only on your device.

If you tap Challenge a friend, your phone's share sheet opens with a message, a picture of the board's stars (without its letters or answers) and a challenge link. You choose the app and the people it goes to. The challenge contains only what your friend needs to play the same board:

  • the letters and hidden words of that board;
  • your time;
  • an optional nickname you type yourself;
  • a random number that identifies this installation of the game, made on your device and used only so the game can tell your own challenges apart and settle replies.

The challenge is carried in the part of the link after the "#", which browsers don't send to web servers. Our challenge page reads it in your browser and doesn't send it anywhere. Replies your friends send back work the same way.

If a friend installs the game from a challenge link, Google Play tells the game which challenge the install came from (the Play Install Referrer) so it can open that challenge. This happens on the device; nothing is sent to us.

Usage statistics

Permissions

The game asks Android for internet and network-state access (for ads, purchases and Google Play Games), the advertising ID (for ads, added by Google's ads SDK), Google Play billing, and vibration (for haptic feedback). It doesn't use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location, and it doesn't send notifications.

Who we share it with

  • Google runs leaderboards and achievements (Google Play Games), serves ads (AdMob) and processes purchases (Google Play), as described above.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

We don't sell your personal information for money. In some places the law counts advertising that uses data like your advertising ID as "selling" or "sharing"; the ad choices above let you opt out of it.

How long it's kept, and deleting it

  • On your device: your save stays until you uninstall the game or clear its storage (Android Settings ▸ Apps ▸ {{name}} ▸ Storage ▸ Clear storage). Either one deletes it for good.
  • Google Play Games: scores, achievements and your Play Games profile are kept by Google. You can delete them from your Google Play Games settings or your Google Account.
  • Ads and purchases: Google keeps ads and purchase data according to its own policies. Your purchase history stays with your Google account so purchases can be restored.
  • Email: if you email us, we keep the conversation only as long as we need to help you, and we'll delete it if you ask.
